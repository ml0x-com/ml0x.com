Support ml0x

I'm Michael Lip, and I build ml0x on my own. I started it in April 2026. In 6 months it has grown to 31 free ML tools and calculators, plus guides and a cheat sheet. About 30 people a month find it on Google.

What it costs

The site runs on GitHub Pages, which is free. I pay for the domain myself. The larger cost is time: the hours to build each tool, check its math and fix what breaks.

Where a tip goes

Tips pay for the domain and buy back hours for new tools and fixes.
