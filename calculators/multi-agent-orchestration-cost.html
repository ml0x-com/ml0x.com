Frequently asked questions

Why is multi-agent orchestration more expensive than a single agent?

Each worker starts with its own cold system prefix and repository context, and the supervisor must read every worker handoff summary to merge results.

What is the ideal number of parallel coding workers?

For most repositories, 3 to 6 independent workers deliver 2.5x to 4x wall-clock speedup before merge conflicts and coordination overhead flatten returns.

How do I cut multi-agent API costs by 50% or more?

Use tiered routing (Opus 4 or Sonnet 4 for planning, Sonnet 4 or Haiku 3.5 for isolated worker execution) and enforce compact handoff reports.
