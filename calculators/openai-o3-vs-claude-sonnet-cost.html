Why can OpenAI o3 cost more than Claude Sonnet 4 even though o3's output price is lower?

OpenAI o3 generates hidden reasoning tokens billed at the $8/M output rate. On hard prompts with a 3x–4x reasoning multiplier, effective output cost reaches $24–$32 per million visible tokens, exceeding standard Sonnet 4 ($15/M).

Which has better prompt caching: OpenAI or Anthropic?

Anthropic offers a 90% discount on cached input reads (0.10x) vs OpenAI's 75% discount (0.25x), though Anthropic charges a 1.25x write fee on cache creation.
