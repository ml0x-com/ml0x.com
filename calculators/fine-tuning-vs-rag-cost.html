Frequently asked questions

When is RAG cheaper than fine-tuning?

RAG is cheaper below ~200K–500K queries/month or whenever your knowledge base changes daily, because you avoid dedicated GPU hosting and repeated retraining cycles.

When does fine-tuning beat RAG on cost?

At high query volume (>1M queries/month) with long retrieved contexts (>4,000 tokens/query), fine-tuning a smaller 8B–32B model eliminates the recurring input-token tax.

Should I combine RAG and fine-tuning?

Yes — in production, RAG supplies up-to-date factual grounding while a lightweight LoRA adapter enforces strict JSON schemas, domain tone, and citation behavior.
