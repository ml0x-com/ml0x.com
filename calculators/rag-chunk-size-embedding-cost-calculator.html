Frequently asked questions

What is the best chunk size for RAG in 2026?

For general technical documentation and knowledge bases, 512 tokens with 10%-15% overlap is the strongest starting point. Use 256 tokens for short factoid lookup and 1024 tokens (or parent-child retrieval) for legal, code, and narrative documents.

How does chunk overlap affect embedding cost and vector DB size?

A 20% overlap shrinks your effective stride to 80% of the chunk size, increasing both total chunks and total embedded tokens by 25% (1 / 0.80). Keeping overlap at 10%-15% preserves boundary context without inflating RAM.

How much RAM does an HNSW vector index need?

For FP32 1536-dim vectors, raw vectors take ~6 KB per chunk and HNSW graph links + metadata add ~1.5x overhead (~9 KB/chunk, or ~9 GB per 1 million chunks). Switching to INT8 scalar quantization cuts vector RAM by ~70% with <1% recall loss.

Should I use text-embedding-3-small or text-embedding-3-large?

text-embedding-3-small ($0.02/1M, 1536d) is 6.5x cheaper to embed and uses half the vector RAM of text-embedding-3-large ($0.13/1M, 3072d). If you use 3-large, Matryoshka dimension reduction to 1024 dims recovers most of the RAM savings.

How do I estimate monthly vector database hosting cost?

Managed vector stores (Pinecone, Qdrant Cloud, Turbopuffer, pgvector) scale primarily with active RAM/storage (~$12-$25/GB-month for hot HNSW RAM). Quantizing vectors to INT8 or binary is the fastest way to cut monthly vector DB bills.
