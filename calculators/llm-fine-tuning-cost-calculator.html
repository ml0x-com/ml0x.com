Memory & compute formulas used

  • Full Fine-Tuning: 6 × N × D FLOPs (plus +25% if gradient checkpointing recomputes activations). Memory is ~16 bytes/param (BF16 weights + BF16 grads + FP32 AdamW master/m/v) plus activations.
  • BF16 LoRA: ~4.2 × N × D FLOPs (frozen base skips weight-gradient GEMMs). Memory is ~2.25 bytes/param plus activations.
  • 4-bit QLoRA: ~4.2 × N × D FLOPs at ~0.72x effective MFU due to NF4→BF16 on-the-fly dequantization. Memory is ~0.75 bytes/param plus activations.

Frequently asked questions

How are LLM fine-tuning FLOPs calculated?

Full fine-tuning uses approximately 6 × N × D FLOPs (2N for forward pass, 4N for backward pass over activations and weights), where N is parameter count and D is total training tokens (dataset tokens × epochs). LoRA skips base weight gradients and uses roughly 4.2 × N × D FLOPs, while QLoRA adds ~25–35% dequantization overhead on top of LoRA.

How much VRAM does Full Fine-Tuning require vs LoRA and QLoRA?

Full BF16 fine-tuning with AdamW requires ~16–18 bytes per parameter (2B weights + 2B grads + 12B FP32 master weights and optimizer states) plus activations, or ~1,200 GB for a 70B model. BF16 LoRA needs ~2.3 bytes/param (~165 GB for 70B), and 4-bit QLoRA needs ~0.75 bytes/param (~52 GB for 70B), fitting on a single 80 GB GPU.

Why is QLoRA sometimes slower per token than BF16 LoRA?

QLoRA must dequantize 4-bit NF4 base weights to BF16 during every forward and backward pass, reducing effective MFU by roughly 25–35%. However, because QLoRA requires far fewer GPUs, it often costs less in total cloud dollars when you would otherwise have to rent an 8× GPU node.

What Model FLOPs Utilization (MFU) should I expect in practice?

Well-tuned frameworks like Unsloth, Axolotl with FlashAttention-2/3, or Megatron-LM achieve 35%–48% MFU for full fine-tuning on H100/H200, 30%–40% for BF16 LoRA, and 22%–30% for 4-bit QLoRA. Unoptimized PyTorch loops often sit around 15%–25%.

How many epochs should I fine-tune an LLM?

For instruction or chat SFT, 2–3 epochs is standard; beyond 3–4 epochs, models rapidly overfit and lose general reasoning. For continued pretraining on domain corpora, 1 epoch is usually optimal.
