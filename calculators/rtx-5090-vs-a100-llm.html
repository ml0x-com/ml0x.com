Frequently asked questions

Is an RTX 5090 faster than an A100 80GB for LLM inference?

For models that fit inside 32 GB (like Qwen3-32B Q4_K_M or Llama 3.3 8B–32B), the RTX 5090's 1,792 GB/s bandwidth and native FP4 tensor cores deliver ~85–110% of an A100 80GB's single-stream decode speed.

Where does the A100 80GB still beat the RTX 5090?

VRAM capacity (80 GB vs 32 GB) and NVLink multi-GPU interconnect. A 70B INT4 model (~40 GB) fits on one A100 80GB but requires two RTX 5090 cards over PCIe.

How many hours per day justify buying an RTX 5090 over renting cloud A100s?

At 6–8 hours/day of active inference or fine-tuning, a single RTX 5090 rig typically reaches full payback against cloud A100 80GB rentals in 6 to 9 months.
