Frequently asked questions

What is the best default RAG chunk size in 2026?

512 tokens with 10% to 15% overlap is the strongest general baseline. Technical docs and codebases often perform better at 384 to 512 tokens paired with a cross-encoder reranker.

Does embedding cost or LLM generation cost matter more in RAG?

LLM generation input tokens usually exceed embedding costs by 50x to 500x. Cutting top-K from 12 to 6 via reranking saves far more than switching embedding models.

How much overlap should I use between RAG chunks?

10% to 15% overlap prevents splitting key sentences across chunk boundaries without inflating vector storage or retrieval duplication.
