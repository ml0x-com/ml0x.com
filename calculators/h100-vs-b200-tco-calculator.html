Frequently asked questions

Is NVIDIA B200 cheaper per token than H100 over 3 years?

Yes. Even though an 8x B200 HGX server costs ~50%-60% more upfront than an 8x H100 SXM node, B200 delivers 2.4x higher HBM bandwidth (8 TB/s vs 3.35 TB/s) and native FP4 support, cutting effective $/1M token TCO by 35%-55%.

How much power does an 8x B200 node use compared to 8x H100?

An 8x H100 SXM server draws roughly 10.2 kW at peak load (700W per GPU plus CPUs/NVSwitch), whereas an 8x B200 HGX node draws ~12.5-14.3 kW (1000W per GPU). Over 3 years at $0.14/kWh and 1.3 PUE, power represents ~10%-15% of total TCO.

What is the effective $/GPU-hour of owning H100 vs B200?

Amortized over 3 years at 65% utilization including power, cooling, and networking, an owned H100 SXM typically lands around $1.90-$2.30/effective GPU-hr, while a B200 lands around $2.85-$3.40/effective GPU-hr — well below on-demand cloud rates.

When does H100 still make more sense than B200?

H100 wins on short-term lead times, existing air-cooled 10 kW racks that cannot support liquid-cooled Blackwell densities, or secondary-market discounts where 8x H100 nodes trade below $220K.

Does B200 reduce the number of GPUs needed for large MoE models?

Yes. With 192GB HBM3e per GPU (1.53 TB per 8-GPU node vs 640GB on H100), a single 8x B200 node fits 671B DeepSeek-V3 in FP8 with huge KV cache headroom, eliminating multi-node InfiniBand latency.
