Frequently asked questions

When is B200 cheaper per million tokens than H200?

B200 wins on $/1M tokens whenever its hourly rental premium over H200 is smaller than its throughput-per-dollar advantage. At equal FP8 precision, B200 offers 1.67x the HBM bandwidth and 2.27x the dense FP8 FLOPS of H200; when using native FP4 on B200, B200 can stay cheaper even at 2.2x-2.8x the hourly H200 price.

Why does B200 reduce the number of GPUs required for DeepSeek-V3 or Llama 4 Maverick?

B200 packs 192 GB of HBM3e per GPU versus 141 GB on H200. Combined with native FP4 weights (~0.55 bytes/param including scales), a 671B model takes ~370 GB of weights and fits cleanly on 4x B200 (768 GB total), whereas FP8 on H200 requires 8x H200 (1,128 GB total).

How does batch size affect the B200 vs H200 comparison?

At batch size 1-8, inference is strictly memory-bandwidth bound, so B200's advantage tracks its 8.0 vs 4.8 TB/s (1.67x) bandwidth ratio. Above batch 32-64, attention and FFN GEMMs become compute-bound, where B200's 2.27x FP8 and 4.5x FP4 Tensor Core advantage kicks in.

Does this calculator account for NVLink tensor-parallel overhead?

Yes. Multi-GPU tensor parallelism applies an efficiency factor (0.94 at TP=2, 0.89 at TP=4, 0.82 at TP=8 on H200; slightly higher on B200 due to 1,800 GB/s NVLink5) so cluster throughput reflects real-world all-reduce communication overhead.

Should I rent B200s or use hosted LLM APIs?

Dedicated GPU nodes only beat hosted APIs when your sustained utilization exceeds ~40-60% across the month. For bursty or sub-200M token/month workloads, compare your $/1M result here against our LLM API cost calculator.
