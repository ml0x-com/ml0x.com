Frequently asked questions

Can I run Ollama in production?

Ollama works well for internal tools with 1–5 concurrent users or asynchronous single-worker queues. Above ~8 concurrent users, Ollama's lack of continuous batching causes P95 latency spikes.

How much higher is vLLM throughput than Ollama?

Under 32–64 concurrent requests, vLLM typically delivers 4x to 10x higher aggregate tokens/second on the same GPU thanks to PagedAttention and iteration-level continuous batching.

Does vLLM support GGUF models like Ollama?

vLLM supports GGUF experimentally, but for peak production throughput you should serve FP8, AWQ, or GPTQ safetensors checkpoints.
