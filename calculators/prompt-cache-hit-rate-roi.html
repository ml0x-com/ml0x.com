Frequently asked questions

What is the exact break-even hit rate for Anthropic 5-minute caching?

With a 1.25x cache write and 0.10x cache read, break-even is 21.7% (roughly 1 cache hit per 3.6 writes). Above 80% hit rate, input spend drops by more than 65%.

When is 1-hour prompt caching worth the 2.0x write fee?

1-hour caching breaks even at a 52.6% hit rate. It pays off for bursty developer sessions or background agents that pause 10 to 45 minutes between turns.

What silently invalidates prompt caches in coding agents?

Inserting live timestamps into the system prompt, toggling MCP servers mid-session, or editing CLAUDE.md every turn forces a full cache rewrite.
