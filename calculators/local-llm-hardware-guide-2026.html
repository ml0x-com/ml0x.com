Frequently asked questions

Should I buy an RTX 5090 PC or a Mac Studio M4 Ultra for local LLMs?

Buy an RTX 5090 (or 2x 5090) if you want 2x–3x faster decode speeds on 8B–70B models and CUDA fine-tuning. Buy a Mac Studio M4 Ultra (192GB or 512GB) if you want to load 109B–671B MoE models quietly at <180W.

How much electricity does a dual RTX 5090 rig use?

Under load, a 2x RTX 5090 workstation draws ~950–1,100W (~$25–$40/month at 6 hrs/day and $0.16/kWh), compared to ~160W (~$5/month) on a Mac Studio.

What is the sweet spot model size for 32 GB VRAM in 2026?

Qwen3-32B (Q4_K_M, ~19 GB) and Qwen3-30B-A3B MoE (~18 GB) are the top 32 GB local models, leaving ample headroom for 32K+ context windows.
