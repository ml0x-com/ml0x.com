Frequently asked questions

Why is B200 so much faster than H200 on LLM inference?

B200 pairs 8.0 TB/s of HBM3e bandwidth (1.67x H200's 4.8 TB/s) with native NVFP4 tensor cores, halving weight bytes read per decode step and cutting minimum GPU count on 70B–671B models.

When is H200 still cheaper per token than B200?

When spot or reserved H200 pricing drops below half the B200 hourly rate and your workload runs FP8 at low batch sizes where B200's FP4 compute advantage is underutilized.

How many H200 vs B200 GPUs do I need for DeepSeek-V3 671B?

In FP8, DeepSeek-V3 needs ~700 GB for weights plus KV cache, requiring 8x H200 (1,128 GB total). In NVFP4 on B200, weights drop to ~370 GB, fitting cleanly on 4x B200 (768 GB total).
