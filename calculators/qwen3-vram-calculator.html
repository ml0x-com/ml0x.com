Frequently asked questions

Does Qwen3-32B fit on an RTX 5090 (32 GB)?

Yes! At Q4_K_M (~19 GB weights), Qwen3-32B fits comfortably on a single RTX 5090 with room for 32K–64K context at ~70+ tok/s.

Why is Qwen3-30B-A3B so fast compared to Qwen3-32B?

Qwen3-30B-A3B only activates 3.3B parameters per token instead of 32.8B, reading 10x less weight data from VRAM per decode step and hitting 200–400+ tok/s on consumer GPUs.

How much VRAM does Qwen3-235B-A22B need?

At INT4/Q4_K_M, weights take ~136 GB plus KV cache, fitting on a Mac Studio M4 Ultra 192GB, 1x B200 (192 GB), or 2x H100/H200.
