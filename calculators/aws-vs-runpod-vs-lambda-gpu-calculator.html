Frequently asked questions

How much cheaper are RunPod and Lambda than AWS EC2 for H100 GPUs?

On-demand 8x H100 SXM instances on AWS EC2 (p5.48xlarge) list around $6.80-$8.50/GPU-hr (after recent price cuts, still ~$6.88/GPU-hr on-demand), whereas Lambda Cloud, RunPod Secure Cloud, and CoreWeave price H100 SXM around $2.49-$3.49/GPU-hr — saving 50%-65% before egress.

Why does data egress matter so much on AWS?

AWS charges ~$0.09/GB ($90/TB) for internet egress, whereas RunPod, Lambda, and Vast.ai include free or near-zero egress. Moving 10 TB/month of checkpoints or datasets out of AWS adds ~$900/mo on top of GPU compute.

When is AWS EC2 still worth the higher GPU hourly rate?

AWS wins when you have committed EDP enterprise spend credits, strict VPC/HIPAA/FedRAMP compliance boundaries, or petabytes of training data already sitting in S3 in the same region.

What is the difference between RunPod Community Cloud and Secure Cloud?

Secure Cloud runs in Tier-3/4 enterprise datacenters with verified uptime and compliance, while Community Cloud (and Vast.ai) uses vetted peer hosts at lower hourly rates — ideal for fault-tolerant fine-tuning or batch inference.

How much do 1-year reserved GPU contracts save?

1-year reserved commitments typically save 25%-35% across both AWS Savings Plans and neoclouds like Lambda and CoreWeave.
